⚖️ Refund Rules & Audit Safeguards

  • Eligibility Check: Transactions with status PENDING or FAILED cannot be refunded.
  • Duplicate Prevention: Prevents multiple concurrent refund requests for the same payment.
  • Cross-File State Synchronization: Approving a refund automatically changes:
    • refunds.txt status to REFUNDED
    • payments.txt status to REFUNDED
    • receipts.txt status to REFUNDED
  • Permanent Audit Trail: Timestamp and reason are permanently preserved in the data files.
